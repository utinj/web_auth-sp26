The Catalogue

Preface

This page contains images of various elements and assets from my various video game projects. Here you can get an overview of what it looks like to contruct a video game, the parts that make up its graphics and designs, and how it comes together to make a cohesive experience.

Most, if not all assets that make up the visual and level design of games come from outside the game engine, created by artists to be placed within the game, including both 2D and 3D art. This means that much of the catalogue is moreso focused on the art aspects of creating games, rather than the programming. Still, art is half the battle when it comes to video games. After all, programming can only define the rules of the game, but art constitutes the video portion of video games.

The catalogue is organized into groups based on the type and purpose of each asset. Within each group, the assets are somewhat ordered by date of creation and project of origin, showing the progression over the years in the way I create my art.
