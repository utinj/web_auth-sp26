About

Meet the team!

Utinj
Founder, Lead Director

Utinj is the founder and lead director of the TinjTeam development studio. A game developer and creative at heart, his team has created many games, which are showcased here in GameTionery. As a programmer, writer, and pixel art enthusiast, he spearheadeds fun and snappy games such as MiniShootArena!

He also created the GameTionery website, showing insights and tips on how our games are made.

Kitsunix
Artist

Kitsunix is our lead artist, who has helped create many of the art assets you see on the site today. She has experience in traditional and digital art, and is now experimenting with 3D modeling! She also helped with some of the design of the GameTionery site.

Jyach
Assistant Programmer, Designer

Jyach is our assistant programmer and designer for our games. He has provided backend support, and helped devise concepts and refined our game design. With a forward looking mind, he creates many of the initial drafts in the conceptual phase of our game creation process. He has also given writing assistence for the creation of this website.

Illumi
Webmaster, Network Programmer

Illumi is our webmaster, managing and maintaining the GameTionery website. He assisted in the creation of the website, including making and refining the CSS. Having experience in Internet technologies, he also works on the server based multiplayer of some of our games.

Timeline

2023

TinjTeam is founded by Utinj, and we create our first game: MiniShootArena! It is a simple top down, 2D multiplayer shooter. As his first game development project, it is made crudely with heart. It was also our introduction to Godot. Jyach would join the team during the development of MiniShootArena.

2024

We participated in two game jams this year! From these we have made the games Nine to NaN and UmaTwo. This was also the time where we began work on the GameTionery website, a place to showcase our process and what goes behind making such games to video game enthusiasts and upincoming game developers. This was also the year when Kitsunix and Illumi joined the team.

2025

More work is done on the website, and the team begins work on our latest project, code named SundLight. Our games and assets were compiled and placed into the site.

2026

The GameTionery website is released to the public! Our team continues to update the site with our latest developments, and work continues on our next project.
